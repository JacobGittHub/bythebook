Book UI · Q10 · second levels

Library Zoom

Opening a book's page first zooms the camera above the book in on the right page, then turns a leaf. The second level's pages are laid out for the zoomed view, so their type reads at the usual size, and the book runs on below the window with no scroll. Going back turns the leaf, then zooms out. Turn, then zoom is the earlier version with a scrolling camera, and Slide (D8) is the plan as it stands.

What the plan would change

  • D8, D9. A second level zooms the camera in until the right page fills the window and about 290 px of the left page stays in view, then turns a leaf to a spread of the book list (right-aligned toward the spine) and the book's page. Going back turns the leaf back, then zooms out. A Bookstore entry works the same way.
  • D5. "Only a second level moves the book" becomes "only a second level moves the camera". The book itself never changes size or shape.
  • A second level's pages are laid out for the zoomed view. They're drawn at the window's size and set onto the page about 1.6 times smaller, so zoomed in they read like any other page. Only the part in view is laid out, and you never see the rest, since you only see these pages zoomed in.
  • D4. While zoomed in, the Library's bookmark is on the left page, out of view. Its tab in the strip stays lit.
  • The page never scrolls, and neither does the camera. The strip stays where it is, off the book.

What to look for

  • Zooming into text. The first level's right page is magnified as the camera comes in, before the leaf covers it. Overlapped (the default) starts the turn 45% of the way into the zoom (and going back, the zoom-out 45% into the turn); One after the other doesn't. Fast (the default) shortens both.
  • The page holds more. The book's page now has the whole window, so the tree view and the board get room the spread page doesn't have.
  • The one-page form (phones and narrow windows) has no room to zoom, so a second level stays a turn to its own page (D15).
  • Cost. The zoom is one transform on the book, the cheapest kind of animation. Text is redrawn sharp once it stops. Reduced motion and None change the page and move the camera at once.
