The cover, on dark walnut

Round two's B1 made concrete. The closed book signs you in, using the app's own cover, paper, fields and buttons. The invitation and the index card lie tucked under the book's right side, so you can see they're there, and the invitation lies mostly over the index card. The page

Signing in

Try the links under the fields, or click a sheet itself. “Back to signing in” tucks the sheet away again. Submitting the invitation makes the account and opens the book. Submitting the index card returns you to the cover with a note.

  • Closer to the app: the cover is the book's own --cover with its radius and shadow, and the page edges are the app's stripe. The panel, the fields and the buttons are the app's Panel, Input and Button, with the same three fonts. The look shouldn't change between signing in and the first page.
  • Less cartoon: no outlined mugs or sticky notes. The desk holds things a chess player has: closed books, a scoresheet, a pencil, a pawn, a cup ring, a paper clip. Each one sits partly out of the frame, as in a photograph, and lies under the book and sheets, so it never covers text.
  • A little different each visit: each corner of the desk draws from a fixed set of objects and spots, and the scoresheet picks one of four real lines. A seed chooses them, so a test can pin it and the page never shifts while you're on it. “Another visit” rolls it again.
  • On a phone the book sits near the top, and the two sheets peek up from the bottom of the screen.

Later, as a vibe

The book comes alive

Yes, this is possible, and it's not much work. Each effect is a short CSS animation on parts the book already has, and a small timer picks when to play one. These four are drawn from Riddle's diary and the Monster Book. Under reduced motion none of them run.

  • Only while idle: never while a field has focus or the pointer is moving, never during a page turn, and at most once a visit. In the app it would wait for about half a minute of stillness, not 7 seconds.
  • Where it belongs: a vibe (plans/vibes.md). Vibes D13 already says ornaments are decoration only and stop under reduced motion. One rule is new: a rattle moves the whole book, where vibes so far only paint it, so a vibe would need permission to move the book while it's idle.
  • Inside the app the same effects could play on the open book. For example, light could leak from the page edges when you finish drilling a line, or the bookmarks could flutter. A tie to something you did will probably feel better than a random one.
