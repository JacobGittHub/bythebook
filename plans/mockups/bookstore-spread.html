The Bookstore's book page: layout B, with your changes

B now has "In this book" on the left page, above one section that switches between Reviews and Leaderboard. "None yet" shows that section before anyone has reviewed the book or joined the leaderboard, which is how it starts. The way back to the shelf is a page ear in the top-left corner, with a word on the fold and paper under it; reviews are listed here but written from the book's page in your library; "Link" shows the old text link instead. The right page keeps the board and the drawing, and the arrow keys walk the drawing after a click anywhere on the mock. A, C and "Like the Library" stay for comparison. The book is the real Italian Game example book. The ratings, reviews, players and every master's number are made up.

B · Readers, your pick

  • In this book sits under the title block and the facts line: each opening the book names, in tree order, in a short box that scrolls. Clicking one takes the board and the drawing there.
  • Reviews and Leaderboard share one section below it, switched with the tabs in its head, and it takes the rest of the page. A review is up to 200 characters, one per signed-in reader, and is written from the book's page in their library, not here (D22, as amended).
  • The leaderboard ranks players who chose to show their drill stats (D10, D18): lines known, accuracy and drills, this month or all time. It stays marked "Comes with the trainer" until drills exist.
  • None yet is what every book shows at first: no rating, and a line in place of each list saying how it fills.

Page ears

  • Under the fold is paper, the next leaf, shaded toward the crease.
  • A first level has none. A Library book's page has one at the top left (back to the Library) and one at the top right (on to its reviews and leaderboard). A store book's page has only the top-left one. The Library's third level has a two-fold ear (book-ui.md D21).
  • A word on every fold, since a control that routes says so in words, and the corner lifts a little on hover. The two-fold ear's outer fold goes back one level and its inner fold two (book-ui.md D22).

A · The book first

  • A title page: what the book covers, its title, a subtitle, the publisher and rating, and Save to library, then a short blurb within D8's 200 characters.
  • About the publisher has the publisher's mark, verification, a line about them and three more of their books. Publishers would need a short bio, which bookstore.md doesn't have yet.

C · Played by masters

  • Each master gets a percentage: how many of their moves as White, while still in the book's lines, the book has. It also shows how far they stay in the book on average. The closest master is named at the top.
  • Picking a master paints the drawing with their games, as "Your games" paints yours, and the moves beside the board count their games.
  • The data needs games tagged by player, which position_cache doesn't have: Lichess's masters explorer can't filter by player. Collections of master games (by player or by event) would be read once by a script, like the example books. Their licences need checking first, and this belongs in a plan of its own.
