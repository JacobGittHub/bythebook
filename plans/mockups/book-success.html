A book's own page, with your games

The Italian Game example book's own page in the Library, opened in ply columns (D31) with "Your games" on, laid out by D36 (facts beside the title) with success as color and thickness (D35). The tree is live: click it, then use the arrow keys or drag it (D32); a row of "Where you leave this book" jumps to its position. The book's facts are the ones the Bookstore questions propose. The book is real; the games, rating and reviews are made up.

The page (D36)

  • The facts fill the space beside the title, like a book's front matter. The page scrolls, so below them the drawing, the board and "Where you leave this book" are seen together. The choices sit in one row just above the drawing.
  • The panel by the board keeps its size: "Next in the book" and "Left the book here" are boxes of fixed height that scroll inside, so moving through the tree never moves the page.
  • "Where you leave this book" lists every place and scrolls in its own box.
  • "Games: All 1,160 as White" opens the checkboxes (Q22): Bullet, Blitz, Rapid, Classical and Daily each on their own, the sources with their imports, and the dates. It opens a panel, not a menu, so it has no arrow.

Success on ply columns (D35)

  • The line into each position takes your score's color, and its thickness is the games that went that way, so the drawing shows the flow of your games through the book. Lines no game reached stay faint.

The book's facts (bookstore.md D17, Q6–Q8)

  • Difficulty (D17) runs from 0.0 to 5.0, color coded from green through gold to deep red. The publisher's comes first; the measured one, from the moves to remember and the length of the lines, sits beside it, with both marked on the scale.
  • Rating, saves and reviews come from the store book this copy was saved from, read live (Q6, Q7).
  • Coverage is the share of master games that stay in the book to a leaf (D11), always beside the lines' length.
  • Zoom now sits with the view's other controls, at the end of the row above the drawing.
