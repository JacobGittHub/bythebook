Clashes

  • A clash is a position where the book plays more than one move for your side. The count is clickable and opens the dialog.
  • In the dialog each clash shows its board with an arrow for every ticked move, and each move's lines, your games and your score. A clash is resolved when one move is ticked. One move always stays ticked.
  • The flag on the count ("9 to resolve") shows while any clash has two or more ticked, so a book you haven't trimmed says so. A clash under an unticked move doesn't count: drills never reach it.
  • Unticked moves stay in the book. The drawing dashes them, the contents greys their openings, and "Next in the book" tags them "not drilled". Drills skip them. Editing the book is still a separate action.
  • Leaderboards, which you mentioned, would have to compare players who drill different parts of a book. That's left for the leaderboard's plan.

Facts

  • Difficulty is the scale alone. Hovering or focusing it shows the publisher's rating and the measured one, and what the measure counts.
  • The measure comes from moves to remember and the average line, so both sit beside the scale on one row.
  • Positions, lines with the longest, coverage and Clashes share the row below.

Your games, and the rest

  • The games filter opens on the last 12 months, as the history page does, with 3 months, 12 months and all time at its top. The tree, the list and the totals follow it.
  • Switch book is gone. "Back to all books" returns to the Library with this book picked, and you choose another there.
  • The arrow keys work after a click anywhere on the page. The box beside the board follows the ringed move, and the board's arrow shows the move the keys would play, or the hovered one.
