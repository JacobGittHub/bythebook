The game history page

Laid out by D30: the games on the left page, filtered by site, time, side and import, with the games through the current position; the board and the games' tree on the right, in ply columns (D31). The tree is live: click it, then use the arrow keys (D32) or drag it. The filters and imports change the tree too. Games, ratings and dates are made up; opponents' names are never kept (D8).

The left page: which games

  • Filters by site, time class, side, exact time control and dates, with the imports beside them: each import can be left out, so a tournament file or an old account can be looked at alone.
  • The totals bar counts what the filters and imports leave.
  • Games through the current position, newest first, each linking to its game on its site. At the starting position it is every game.

The right page: where they went

  • The board shows the current position, its results and the moves played next; "Open in Explorer" sits in the box's corner.
  • The tree is ply columns, each line as thick as the games that went that way. Color switches between the book views' own paint and your score (red at 30% or less, grey at 50%, green at 70% or more).
  • Zoom sits with the view's other controls, above the drawing.

Walking the tree (D32)

  • ↑ ↓ move the dashed ring among the current position's moves, stopping at the first and last.
  • → plays the ringed move; ← goes back, with the ring on the move you came from.
  • The camera pans to keep the current position and its moves in view; drag to look elsewhere. "Fit" shows the whole tree.
