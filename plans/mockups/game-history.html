The game history page

Laid out by D30, with the way of choosing games proposed in Q22: the side beside the title, Time and From as buttons that open checkbox panels, and a strip of months to drag across. The board and the games' tree are on the right, in ply columns (D31). The tree is live: click it, then use the arrow keys, drag it, or zoom with Ctrl and the wheel (D32 as amended). Games, ratings and dates are made up; opponents' names are never kept (D8).

The left page: which games (Q22)

  • One kind of control per kind of choice. The side is the only either/or, since the tree is drawn from one side, so it is a toggle beside the title. Every other choice is checkboxes, all ticked to start, each with its games. Dates are a span.
  • Time is one list: each class with its exact time controls beneath it. A class is part-ticked when only some of its controls are.
  • From folds the site and the imports together: each site with its imports beneath it, so a tournament file or an old account can be looked at alone.
  • The strip of months shows when you played and chooses the span: drag across it, or pick 3 months, a year or all.
  • The buttons say what is chosen and open panels, not menus, so they have no arrow. Counts in a panel are under the other choices.

The right page: where they went

  • The board shows the current position, its results and the moves played next. The details keep the board's height: the moves played next scroll in their own box, so a busy position never moves the tree.
  • The tree is ply columns, each line as thick as the games that went that way. Your score is a switch, on to start: red at 30% or less, grey at 50%, green at 70% or more. Off, the lines take the book views' own paint.
  • Zoom runs from 50% to 400% in finer steps on − and +, with Fit for the whole tree.

Walking the tree (D32)

  • ↑ ↓ move the dashed ring among the current position's moves, stopping at the first and last.
  • → plays the ringed move; ← goes back, with the ring on the move you came from.
  • The camera is eager: it keeps the current position a third in from the left and re-centres its moves as soon as they near an edge. It zooms out when the moves don't fit and in when they would sit small, up to 200%.
  • Below 100% the moves you can choose are labelled beside their dots at full size, and the ring keeps its size, so walking still works zoomed out.
