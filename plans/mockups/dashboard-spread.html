The Spread, page by page

The dashboard as an open book, after your notes on round four. The strip is one row again, with the tabs at the left. Each page carries its own bookmark, which meets its tab when the page turns to it. The spine control is gone; only the Library and Bookstore slide the book. The one-page form has its spine on the right and tabs cut into its pages. The figures are sample numbers.

Things to try

  • Click a tab. The page that turns carries its bookmark: going forward, the new page's bookmark arrives on the leaf; going back, the old one leaves on it. The tab lights as the leaf lands.
  • In the Library, press Open book page. The whole book slides left and the bookmark stays under its tab.
  • Open a Bookstore entry. The book slides the same way, so the shelf's page is out of sight but for a strip at the edge that goes back to the shelf.
  • In the Explorer, press Swap with board at the top right of the right page. The button stays where it is, and says Swap back.
  • Open Visualizations, the chapter back in the strip, and pick a visualization.
  • Switch the form to Single page and pick a tab. The page turns on its spine at the right and takes its tab with it.
  • Turn on Vibes at the top right. Only the book changes. In the one-page form the current page's own tab takes the page's paper; the tab column stays as it is.

What changed since round four

  • One strip: the brand and the tabs at the left, the vibes switch and the account links at the right.
  • The bookmark belongs to the page, not the strip. It doesn't slide; it turns with its page.
  • The spine control and the flat pages are gone. The Bookstore entry slides the book like the Library, which fixes the shelf's page showing beside it.
  • The Explorer's swap button keeps one place, the Labyrinth tab says coming soon, and the move list under the right page is gone; the scoresheet in the margin is the one.
  • Visualizations is a chapter again, and the Overview has eight chapters, with Vibes coming soon.
  • The one-page form has a spine on the right, turns toward it, and its contents are page tabs.

The animations

Which ones this page can show, and what only the real app can answer.

Page turnYes, three waysA rigid leaf with shading; a bending sheet would need WebGL. How it feels on the fiftieth click, and on a cheap laptop or phone.
RiffleYesWhether a jump of several pages reads as "far away" or as lag.
Book slideYesA second level's page lays out once, at its final size, and the slide only moves it. That should be cheap, but check it with the real book view.
BookmarkYesIt is part of the page's picture in a view transition, so it should turn with the leaf for free; the spike checks that the picture includes what sticks out of the page.
Page tabsYesThe same, for the one-page form.
Board swapA fadeWhether the board should glide between pages instead. View transitions can morph it from one place to the other.
