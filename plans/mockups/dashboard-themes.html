Theme directions for ByTheBook

Five looks for the same two pages. Current is what shipped on 2026-10-12. The other four bring back a friendlier, rounder feel in different ways. Each one is only token values plus a few rules on the panels and sidebar, so any of them, or a mix, fits the shadcn/ui setup without moving anything on the page. Pick a direction, switch light and dark, and drag the roundness until it feels right.

The same in every direction

  • The layout. Every box keeps its size and place, so a direction, and later a vibe, only repaints.
  • The board's squares and pieces, and the book views' colors (D11).
  • The move bars stay light, middle and dark (D13).
  • The controls are the same shadcn/ui components. A direction changes the token values in globals.css, plus a few rules on data-slot="panel" and data-slot="sidebar".
  • The sidebar icons are drawn in every direction. They would be a small change to the nav, separate from the theme.

Mixing directions

Each direction turns the same five dials: corners, how boxes separate from each other (lines, soft shadows, a ledge, or glass), the sidebar, the fonts, and the accent color. An answer like "Clubroom's colors with Toybox's buttons" or "Paperback, but rounder" works, since each dial is its own token.

Rounder than Current means lifting the "no corners larger than rounded-lg" rule in AGENTS.md, which was written for the tightening pass.
